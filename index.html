What's Inside

Every calculation a runner needs

From training runs to race day strategy — Pace Calc covers it all, with no login or internet required.

⏱️

Time to Pace

Enter your finish time and distance to instantly calculate your average pace per kilometre or mile.

🏃

Pace to Time

Know your target pace? Calculate the exact finish time for any distance — from a 5 K to an ultramarathon.

⚡

Speed to Pace

Convert km/h or mph to min/km or min/mile in an instant — perfect for treadmill runners.

🔄

Pace to Speed

Go the other way — convert a running pace into speed to match treadmill settings or race data exports.

📏

Pace to Distance

Given a pace and a time, find out exactly how far you will run — great for planning training routes.

📊

Split Strategy

Plan even, negative, or positive splits for your race. Know what pace to hit at every kilometre marker.

🏅

Benchmark Calculations

Compare your performance against common race benchmarks and see how you stack up against personal goals.
